### Consent-gating readout — PRE-REGISTERED 2026-06-12 (CEO review D4; read against these, not after the fact)

Metric formulas (all from server-emitted `companion_turn` rows; session = one companion lesson):
- **violation_rate** = sessions with any turn where `reveals_key_insight=true` AND `escalation_consented=false` AND learner_state ≠ understands ÷ all sessions. Target: 0. ANY violation ⇒ pull transcript, add eval case, prompt patch before further ship.
- **offer_acceptance_rate** = offers accepted ÷ offers made (turn t has `offer_made=true`, turn t+1 has `escalation_consented=true`).
- **bail_after_offer** = sessions whose LAST event is an unanswered offer ÷ sessions with ≥1 offer.
- **rung1_specificity** = distribution of `specificity_level` on each session's first companion reply (the variable customer_conversations.md:226 was actually about — CEO outside-voice finding 1).
- **viz_exposure_rate** = sessions with ≥1 canvas mount ÷ sessions with ≥5 turns (moat-throttle check, outside-voice finding 4).

Decision rules (pre-committed; minimum N = 50 sessions before ANY threshold is read — below that, read transcripts by hand, they're anecdotes):
- offer_acceptance > 80% with near-instant accepts ⇒ offers are a verbalized more-button (the 06-09 fear was right) ⇒ revert offer cadence to stall-only, re-read after 50 more sessions.
- bail_after_offer > 25% ⇒ consent friction too high ⇒ allow ONE unsolicited Level-2 nudge after an ignored offer (single-step softening), NOT a return to full model-pacing.
- viz_exposure_rate < 40% ⇒ the gate is starving the moat ⇒ offers lead with the visual rung in ALL cases, structure view mounts by default at first concrete reference.
- rung1_specificity median > 2 ⇒ opening hints too specific (the actual customer complaint) ⇒ tighten opener doctrine + eval case 1, independent of consent-gating.
- Day-1 PostHog panels (5): violation_rate, offer_acceptance_rate, bail_after_offer, rung1_specificity distribution, viz_exposure_rate.
